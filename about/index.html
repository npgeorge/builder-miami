About
Builder Miami.

Builder Miami is an open conversation about what’s happening in AI and Bitcoin, what it means for builders, and where the two might come together.

We bring together Bitcoin builders, AI builders, and curious newcomers to explore recent developments, share perspectives, and find ideas worth building. Real products, announcements, research, and debates give us a starting point. The conversation follows the questions and experiences of the people in the room.

Five themes to explore

  • BuildHow AI is changing what people can create and who gets to create it.
  • OwnWhat it means to control your tools, data, and digital life.
  • DelegateHow personal agents could act on our behalf, and what trust they need to earn.
  • GovernWho shapes AI’s development, access, and accountability.
  • ConnectWhere Bitcoin payments, open networks, and AI could meet to solve real problems.

The aim is to understand what’s changing, question the assumptions, and turn promising connections into practical experiments. Bring a perspective, a question, or something you’re working on. You don’t need to be an expert in either field to take part.
